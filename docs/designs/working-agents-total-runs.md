## Problem Statement

Cheese's request (verbatim): "thêm tổng runs vào góc trên bên phải của popup này" — add the total run count to the top-right corner of the "N active agents" hover card.

The card lists each agent with its own "N runs" count, but the reader has to add the rows up to know how many runs are active in the surface.

## What exists today (verified 2026-10-29 at `ffa2f9711`)

- The card is `WorkingAgentsHoverContent` (`packages/views/issues/components/workspace-agent-working-chip.tsx`). The workspace "agents working" chip and the sub-issues chip both render it.
- Each row shows `agent.running_task_count` with the `agent_activity.tasks_count` key ("1 run" / "4 runs"), right-aligned, muted, `tabular-nums`.
- The header shows `agent_activity.hover_header` ("6 active agents") alone.

## Premises

1. Total = sum of `running_task_count` over the listed agents.
2. The total shows only for a non-empty roster. The unresolved and empty states have no header and get no total.
3. The per-issue popover (`issue-agent-header-chip.tsx`) is out of scope.

## Approaches Considered

- **A (chosen):** header becomes `justify-between`; the right side shows the total with the existing `tasks_count` key, same style as the row counts.
- B: same position with `hover_header_tasks` ("11 active runs"). Rejected: longer, repeats "active", can wrap in the `w-72` card.
- C: total on the chip trigger. Rejected: not the requested place; widens the toolbar chip.

## Recommended Approach

Approach A. One component, no locale changes; the total sits above the per-row count column and uses the same unit.

## Success Criteria

- A roster of agents with 1 and 3 runs shows "2 active agents" on the left and "4 runs" on the right of the header.
- Unresolved and empty states are unchanged.
- Existing chip and hover tests stay green.
