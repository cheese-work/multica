v2: Workspace and Personal are two tabs (Cheese, option A). This tab sits in Settings → Workspace group and sets the picks for everyone. Owners and admins edit; members see it read-only (frame D2). Scope is always visible: the "Workspace" badge sits next to the title. Rows are one shared component: Job, Agent, Fallback agent. Unset means "No agent" everywhere. Left: 900 px column.
