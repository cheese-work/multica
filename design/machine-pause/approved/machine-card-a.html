A · Machine card · v2 (expiry added)

A new "Machine" card between the machine header and the Runtimes list. Status and the one main action sit on one row; Update and Delete runtime keep their existing buttons in the card footer. The owner banner sits above the page content. v2 adds the recorded expiry rules (CHE-2429): a maintenance pause ends after 2 h, an incident or suspended pause after 30 min. The reason menu names each limit, the card and the banner show the time left, and states 8 and 9 show "ending soon" and "ended". Pause rights: machine owner and workspace owner. Left: desktop 780 px.
