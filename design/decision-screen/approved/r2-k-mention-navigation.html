(k) Mention navigation · what you see

  • The CHE-2116 "jump to my next mention" control grows to first, previous, next, last and a count (3/7). It sits in the sticky issue header (desktop) and in a 44 px row under the top bar (phone).
  • Order: oldest first. First and previous are disabled on 1/n; next and last on n/n. Zero mentions: no control.
  • A mention inside an open decision card counts. Landing on it scrolls to the card, rings it and makes that decision the active one in the floating card (2/3) (B).
  • One control per target: while the next mention is inside the active decision, the floating card drops Go to full card. Next does that job and its tooltip says so (C).

Components, tokens, i18n

  • Extend the existing CHE-2116 jump-to-next-mention control (file path to confirm in that issue, not read in this round); count and targets from the issue's mention list. Decision cards expose data-decision-id.
  • Tokens: --surface-raised, --surface-border, --primary ring, --info-bg.
  • Keys: issues:mentions.first, .prev, .next, .last, .count ({current}/{total}), .nextInDecision. Shortcuts ] next, [ previous.
  • Count is aria-live="polite"; buttons have names, no icon-only unlabeled controls.
  • Focus: 2 px outline, 2 px offset, token --info, on every control; keyboard order follows reading order.
