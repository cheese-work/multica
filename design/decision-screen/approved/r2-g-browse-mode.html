(g) Browse mode · what you see

  • One card per screen, order nearest deadline then issues blocked. The counter reads 2 of 7.
  • Skip and snooze are the only things a swipe or an arrow does. A pick is a tap on an option plus Lock my pick. The recommendation stays hidden until the lock (round 1, not redrawn).
  • Reopen: the dashed row Back to the one I skipped returns to that card and keeps its place in the queue.
  • Phone: vertical swipe up skips, swipe left snoozes. Buttons always exist (no gesture-only path). A gesture that starts within 24 px of the left edge is ignored, so the iOS back swipe wins.

Components, tokens, i18n

  • packages/views/decisions/decisions-page.tsx gains a Browse tab; card stays decision-request-card.tsx + decision-answer-content.tsx.
  • Tokens: --surface, --surface-border, --warning (skipped row), --info-bg (snooze reveal).
  • New keys: decisions:browse.counter, browse.skip, browse.snooze, browse.backToSkipped, browse.endOfQueue; 6-locale parity (parity.test.ts).
  • Touch targets 44 px on phone; gesture arrows have aria-label; S, O, arrows are shortcuts.
  • Focus: 2 px outline, 2 px offset, token --info, on every control; keyboard order follows reading order.
