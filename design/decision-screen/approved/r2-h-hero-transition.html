(h) Hero transition · what you see

  • Open issue on a browse card: the card shrinks into the floating card over the issue. Same element, same question and options, no reload of the card.
  • Opening any issue that has an open decision for the viewer enters decision mode and runs the same transition from wherever the user was (list, mention, link).
  • Unsent input rides along: the card’s Other box and a half-typed note keep their text through the flight (see (m)).
  • prefers-reduced-motion: the flight becomes a 150 ms fade. Nothing travels.

Components, tokens, i18n

  • New packages/views/decisions/decision-hero.tsx (shared-element transition; CSS view-transition-name: decision-card, fallback to the fade when unsupported).
  • Dock size and easing come from tokens: --surface-raised, --radius-card; no new colours.
  • Keys: decisions:hero.openIssue, hero.skipAnimation (aria-live: “Opened issue CHE-2365 in decision mode”).
  • Screen reader: focus moves to the first option once the card lands; the announcement fires once.
  • Focus: 2 px outline, 2 px offset, token --info, on every control; keyboard order follows reading order.
