What you see

  • One draft object per card. The card's Other box and the thread composer read and write it.
  • The thread composer shows a blue "Linked to decision card" chip and an Answer with Other button.
  • Comment only unlinks: the text goes to the thread as a normal comment, the card stays open.
  • After send: one thread comment tagged "Answered the decision", the card shows the short answered form with Revoke.

Components, tokens, i18n

  • decision-request-card.tsx Other box, issue comment composer, decision-answer-content.tsx (paths from the issue, not read from a checkout)
  • Tokens --info-bg --brand --success --ok-bg
  • i18n decisions.other.shared_draft, decisions.other.answer_with_other, decisions.other.comment_only, decisions.other.linked_chip
  • Build dependency CHE-5517 (live card updates) and the existing draft store from CHE-5452.

States and motion

  • Typing, synced, sent, send failed (draft kept, error under the button), answered elsewhere (card locks, see frame s).
  • Unsent text survives reload and navigation until a send or Discard.
  • No motion beyond a 100 ms fade of the synced text. prefers-reduced-motion: none.
  • Focus: 2 px outline, 2 px offset, token --info, on every control; keyboard order follows reading order.
