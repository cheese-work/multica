What you see

  • Same four-tab frame as the doop MemoryPanel.tsx: tab strip with counts, a list per tab, one primary action per row.
  • Decided shows each answer with its veto deadline in ICT and a Veto button.
  • Rule suggestions need 3 repeats in 30 days. Add proposes a rule. Dismiss hides it.
  • Stats shows four numbers and one bar chart. The weekly review appears as a brief on Monday 09:00 ICT and again at the top of Stats.
  • Focus: 2 px outline, 2 px offset, token --info, on every control; keyboard order follows reading order.

Components, tokens, i18n

  • Pattern from src/components/MemoryPanel.tsx (doop; path from the issue, not read from a checkout); new decisions-review-panel.tsx for Multica
  • Tokens --surface-selected for the active tab, --brand for chart bars, --info-bg
  • i18n decisions.review.tab.pending, .decided, .rules, .stats, decisions.review.add_rule, .dismiss, .weekly_brief
  • Chart has a text summary in aria-label; numbers carry the table, not the colour.

States and motion

  • Empty tab, loading, error with Retry, veto done (row shows Vetoed), rule added.
  • Tab change is instant. Bars draw in 200 ms. prefers-reduced-motion: bars appear at once.
  • Sample numbers are placeholders for the layout, not measured data.
