What you see

  • Three choices only. The third opens an issue search and a status select.
  • The wake time is written out in ICT before you confirm. It is never later than 24 h.
  • A snoozed card sits in a quiet row. Wake now ends it early.
  • The asking agent sees one line on the issue: snoozed until what, and the latest time.

Components, tokens, i18n

  • decision-request-card.tsx action row, decisions-page.tsx queue, issue picker as in the property picker frame (paths from the issue, not read from a checkout)
  • Tokens --surface-raised --info-bg --surface-hover
  • i18n decisions.snooze.30m, decisions.snooze.1h, decisions.snooze.until_issue, decisions.snooze.latest_at, decisions.snooze.wake_now
  • Server clamps every snooze to 24 h. The 7-day expiry clock is untouched.

States and motion

  • Picker, no results, status never reached (wakes at the 24 h cap), snoozed, woken, expired while snoozed.
  • Wake: card returns to the top with a "Back · snooze ended" tag.
  • Sheet slides up 150 ms. prefers-reduced-motion: appears without slide.
  • Focus: 2 px outline, 2 px offset, token --info, on every control; keyboard order follows reading order.
