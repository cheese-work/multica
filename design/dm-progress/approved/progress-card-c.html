C · Attention first: the card opens with the answer, then shows only what needs you

Why v2. Cheese on v1, 4 Nov 2026: "the proposed designs are too slop. need to overhaul". v1 gave every issue the same weight, so a block looked like a routine move. Here the first line is the verdict ("1 blocked, 2 moving"). Issues that need a person (Blocked, In review, Done) open into a tinted block with detail. Routine moves collapse to one line. Each status has its own glyph and word, so colour never works alone. Mock data, ICT times, product tokens, system font.

Build notes. Verdict text comes from fixed i18n templates and enum counts. Order is Blocked, then In review, then Done, then In progress, then the rest. A tinted block appears for at most 3 issues; extra ones fall back to one-line rows. The title is escaped plain text, 120 characters. The caret opens Open issue, Mute updates, Unfollow (REST calls). Cards have no reply box and start no run.
