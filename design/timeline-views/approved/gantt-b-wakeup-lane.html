Gantt v2 · Project, Issues, My Issues (D1)

Fixture: CHE-2797 tree, real ids and titles; status segments, wakeups and the 2859 markers are illustrative. Window: last 30 days, shown here as 1–6 Nov 2026 ICT. Rows follow the parent/child tree. Click a parent row to collapse or expand it. Every bar ends in a final-status icon and every segment of 22 px or more starts with its status icon, so colour is never the only signal.

Direction A · Arrows drawn over the bars

Reads like a classic Gantt: all wakeups visible at once. Cost: crossings grow with the tree, so it suits Project scope (tens of rows) and gets busy on the workspace scope.

Direction B · Wakeup lane under each bar, arrows only for the selected row

Quiet by default: each row gets a 10 px lane with ticks (triangle up = wakes another issue, down = woken by another, ring = self). Select a row to draw its arrows and read its wakeups in an inspector. Scales to the workspace scope; cost: one extra click to see a link.
